Aiko Chat — Delete Your Account

How to permanently delete your Aiko Chat account and what happens to your data.

Delete your account from the app
  1. Open Aiko Chat and make sure you are signed in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm when prompted. Your account is deleted immediately — no waiting period and no separate request.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes your identity from the Aiko Chat server your app is connected to. Your account and messages live on a single server, so this removes everything associated with your account. This action cannot be undone.

DataWhat happens when you delete your account
Account identity — your username, display name, any email address, and your sign-in credentials (password hash and/or any linked Apple, Google, or passkey identifiers)Permanently deleted.
Blocks you createdPermanently deleted.
Messages you sentKept but anonymised — your name is removed and the messages are relabelled "[deleted user]" so the conversation other people saw stays intact, but the messages no longer link to you.
Reports you filedKept but anonymised — retained without your identity so our safety and moderation records remain auditable.

Retention

We do not run automatic expiry — your account and messages are kept until you delete your account. Once you delete it, your account identity is removed straight away. The anonymised message and report records described above are retained indefinitely with no link back to you, so we cannot re-associate them with your identity after deletion.

If you cannot sign in

Deletion is self-service from within the app while signed in. If you have lost access to your account and cannot sign in to delete it yourself, contact the support address listed on Aiko Chat's App Store / Google Play listing and we will help you complete the deletion.
